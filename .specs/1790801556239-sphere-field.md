# Sphere Field visualization

## Summary

A new full-screen visualization: a uniform grid of small dots covers the screen. A handful of
invisible "virtual spheres" drift around. Each sphere bulges toward the viewer — its center is
closest to the screen and its edges fall away — and every dot takes its size and color from how
far the sphere surface under it rises toward the screen. The spheres themselves are never drawn;
you see them only as swelling, tinted patches passing through the dot field.

## Iteration plan

### Iteration 1
- `src/visualizations/SphereField.tsx` (Canvas 2D, like the other non-Three visualizations).
- Dot grid at a fixed spacing, recomputed on resize.
- 7 spheres, random radius (scaled to the screen), slow drifting velocity, bouncing off the edges,
  each with its own slowly shifting hue.
- Per dot, per sphere: height `h = sqrt(1 - (d/R)^2)` inside the sphere (a true hemisphere
  profile: 1 at the center, 0 at the rim). Overlapping spheres combine as `1 - Π(1 - h)`.
- Dot radius scales from a tiny base to near half the grid spacing with the combined height;
  color blends from a dim base toward the height-weighted mix of the spheres' hues, brighter
  as it rises.
- Register in `App.tsx` as "Sphere Field".
