# Tornado fixes

## Summary

Joe's notes on Tornado:
- Multi-vortex tornadoes sometimes appear — always a single vortex.
- Trees lift as solid objects — make them out of tiny triangles that shred to pieces.
- Roofs fling as big chunks — build them from smaller pieces so they shred.
- The camera is sometimes too close to the ground to see what's going on.
- During the fade at the start/end, objects (especially roads) look too bright — glowing in the dark.

## Iteration plan

### Iteration 1
- Remove the multi-vortex / satellite-vortex mode (the dust whirls / suction vortices) so every
  storm is one funnel.
- Trees: crowns built from many small leaf triangles (instanced shards) + trunk/branch segments;
  wind strips leaves progressively into a pooled shard system, then snaps branches/trunk.
- Roofs: subdivide roof planes into small shingle/sheet tiles that peel and shred individually.
- Camera: enforce a minimum elevation angle/height so the ground plan and damage path stay legible.
- Fade: fade the scene itself to black (exposure/lighting/fog all go dark together, no unlit or
  emissive-looking materials) instead of an overlay that leaves roads glowing.
