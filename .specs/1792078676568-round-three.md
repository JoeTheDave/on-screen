# Round three: drop Mandelbulb, Terrain trees as models, RD phases, Tornado, Atomic Bomb, Prism

## Summary

Joe's feedback:
- Drop **Mandelbulb**.
- **Terrain Flight**: the trees (built into the terrain shading) look terrible — revert them and
  implement trees as individual models, unless that would be too slow (then no trees). Fix the
  peaks "melting": parts of large peaks flicker between visible and not visible.
- **Wave Grid**: spheres ~3× larger (small ones get stuck on a single cube) and twice as many.
- **Strange Attractors**: the last change removed all glow and lost the mystique; find the middle
  ground between no glow and wash-out.
- **Reaction Diffusion**: still boring after the initial spread; the churning just makes regions
  of lines and dots. Make it go through phases: the initial spread, then something else spreads
  through and affects what's there, then something else, etc.
- New **Tornado**: random buildings on land, a simulated tornado sweeps through and the wind
  destroys them.
- New **Atomic Bomb**: same idea — an atomic explosion blasting through buildings.
- New visualization about **refraction of light** — scientific and stunning (**Prism**).

## Iteration plan

### Iteration 1
- Remove `Mandelbulb.tsx` + App entry.
- WaveGrid: sphere radius ×3, count 20 → 40, physics re-checked headlessly at the new size.
- StrangeAttractors: keep normal blending for the body (so clumps saturate to color, not white)
  and restore a glow via a stronger, lower-threshold bloom over it.
- TerrainFlight: revert the in-shader trees (keep the new flight controller); fix the peak
  flicker in the march; trees as instanced 3D meshes depth-composited with the raymarch
  (raymarch writes depth), placed around the camera from the CPU height function — or drop
  trees if that can't hold frame rate.
- ReactionDiffusion: a sequenced phase system — successive "agents"/regimes sweep across the
  field as advancing fronts that transform the existing pattern; remove the line/dot churn.
- New `Tornado.tsx`, `AtomicBomb.tsx`, `Prism.tsx`.
