# Touch-ups: Wave Grid, Strange Attractors, Reaction Diffusion

## Summary

Joe's feedback after living with the new set:
- **Wave Grid**: make the cube field a large circle (still rows/columns of cubes, circular
  footprint). Add circular waves (moving inward or outward) to the existing plane waves; waves
  and colors should randomly fade in and out over time so it keeps changing instead of being
  fixed until refresh. Drop 20 spheres onto the moving cubes — they collide, get bumped around,
  and when they roll off the edge and fall below a threshold they respawn above.
- **Strange Attractors**: too bright — clumped particles blow out into a blob of light. Keep
  separated particles clearly visible but stop dense regions from glowing so hard.
- **Reaction Diffusion**: settles into a mostly stagnant state. Add something that keeps it
  flowing and interesting.

## Iteration plan

### Iteration 1
- WaveGrid: circular mask over a square lattice (radius = half the old width); a pool of wave
  sources (plane waves + radial in/out waves) whose amplitudes crossfade as sources are retired
  and replaced every ~10–20 s; palette crossfades on its own timer; 20 spheres with simple
  sphere-vs-cube-top collision (height field), gravity, restitution, friction, respawn when
  y < threshold.
- StrangeAttractors: lower per-streak intensity, tame bloom (higher threshold, lower strength),
  and soften additive build-up so density reads as structure, not a white blob.
- ReactionDiffusion: add a slow advecting flow field (curl-noise drift of the chemicals) plus
  traveling feed/kill parameter waves, so patterns keep migrating and re-forming.
