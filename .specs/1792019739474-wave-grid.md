# Wave Grid visualization

## Summary

A 3D field of 50 × 50 cubes laid out on a plane, viewed from above at an angle. Each cube's height
follows the sum of two sine waves travelling across the grid from two different directions, so
the field ripples with interference patterns. The camera circles the grid continuously.

## Iteration plan

### Iteration 1
- `src/visualizations/WaveGrid.tsx` (Three.js), one `InstancedMesh` of 2,500 cubes.
- Two plane waves with random directions (≥ 45° apart), wavelengths and speeds; cube height =
  sum of the two.
- Cube color follows its height (low hue → high hue), lit by hemisphere + directional light.
- Camera orbits the grid center at a downward angle, distance fitted so the grid fills most of
  the window at any aspect ratio.
- Register in `App.tsx` as "Wave Grid".
