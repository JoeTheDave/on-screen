# 3D Epicycles visualization

## Summary

A 3D take on Fourier Epicycles. Two arms still chain end to end from a fixed center, but each
arm spins around its own randomly oriented axis in 3D space instead of in a flat plane, so the
tip sweeps out a closed space curve (a knot/coil wrapped around a torus-like surface). The trace
is drawn as connected 3D line segments, colored by tip speed like the 2D version. The camera
orbits the shape continuously and bobs in elevation, with distance fog, so the depth reads.

## Iteration plan

### Iteration 1
- `src/visualizations/Epicycles3D.tsx` (Three.js, like OrbitingLights).
- Arm 1 rotates about random unit axis A1, arm 2 (attached at arm 1's tip) about axis A2; the
  axes are kept at least ~35° apart so the curve is never flat. Lengths split 20-50 / 50-80 %
  as in 2D. Coprime rotation counts (6-30 per cycle) so the curve closes exactly at 60 s.
- Whole curve precomputed (fixed sample count) at reset; the drawn trace grows by bumping
  `instanceCount` on a fat-line (Line2) geometry. Vertex colors interpolate between two random
  hues by per-segment speed.
- Arms drawn white with small joint dots; hidden during the 10 s pause; then a fresh shape.
- Camera: continuous orbit around Y plus a slow elevation swing; fog for depth cueing.
- Register in `App.tsx` as "3D Epicycles".
